Why the style exists

Six principles

  • The room is the directory. Architecture is wayfinding — turn, and every pillar is a doorway.
  • The world is data. Open assets plus a manifest; design lives as tokens, not engine code.
  • Contributor-first. Assign a pillar, add a bench, write a plaque — no engine knowledge needed.
  • Accessible by default. Seated, gaze or single-button, motion-optional — a first constraint.
  • Honest by default. Join / Donate disclosed up front. No dark patterns.
  • Open & portable. Open formats, so the same Hall travels — our site, Viverse, Artemis.
Art direction

Two movements, one Hall

The bones

Beaux-Arts

Domes, colonnades, Corinthian columns, coffered ceilings, formal gardens. Governs scale, symmetry, procession, proportion.

The skin & soul

Art Nouveau

Stained-glass oculus, sinuous gilt tracery, botanical line, handcrafted glass & metal. Governs decoration, curve, craft, warmth.

Beaux-Arts sets the room; Art Nouveau dresses it. Structure square, ornament curved.

Light & matter

Sky, light & materials

Sky & light

Golden-hour sunset meets aurora night — the labradorite spectrum thrown across the sky. A sky shifter offers more than one mood. Serene and alive — never gloomy or neon.

Wood carries

Quartersawn oak — floors, benches, shelving. Warm, matte, the room's warmth.

Gold marks

Cast-gold tracery — came, capitals, plaques. Metallic, low-rough, emissive #EEC96B.

Stone holds the light

Labradorite — columns, panels, pool rim. Near-black until the light moves, then a flash. Wants an iridescence approach — confirm vs. the GLB spec.

Geometry & scale

Twelve-sided, seated-first

  • Dodecagon plan — axial and symmetrical.
  • Twelve pillars, one per vertex — a 1:1 map to programs.
  • A wayfinding centre — the room's origin.
  • Seated-first — plaques at seated eye-height, panels within reach.

Building on-style

  • Bake it in, keep it light — bake textures & lighting; small files load for everyone.
  • Gold is the only glow — emissive #EEC96B, selective and precious.
  • Silhouette carries the style — shape first, Art Nouveau detail second.

Keep design as data

From art to data

Colour, type, geometry, motion and material intent live in tokens.json. Material intent maps to glTF/GLB — final PBR is the modelling team's call, on-brand and within the accepted extensions.

The deck, the manifest, and the material author all read from the tokens. Change the token, not the memory of it.

Let's decide today

Open questions

  • Typography — adopt Package A, stay put, or take B / C?
  • Orientation — which pillar faces the entrance, which the stage?
  • Program order — the real 1–12 assignment around the room.
  • Ceiling — coffered dome + stained-glass oculus, or flat gilt tracery?
  • Gardens — formal parterre, a ring, or ambient?
  • Sky shifter — how many moods, authored by whom?
  • Engine + fabric — do the tokens (esp. labradorite) map cleanly to three.js / Babylon and our manifest vs. OMB Fabric JSON?
