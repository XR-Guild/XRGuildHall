XR Guild Hall · Style Onboarding · 01

Two styles, one Hall

The Hall's art direction — a subset of the XR Guild Design System. Two early-1900s movements, each with a job.

The bones

Beaux-Arts

The grand envelope — domes, colonnades, Corinthian columns, coffered ceilings, carved friezes, symmetrical formal gardens. Governs scale, symmetry, procession, proportion.

The skin & soul

Art Nouveau

The ornament and interiors — stained-glass oculus, sinuous gilt tracery, botanical line, handcrafted glass & metal. Governs decoration, curve, craft, warmth.

Beaux-Arts sets the room; Art Nouveau dresses it. Art nouveau, labradorite and gilt — structure straight & symmetrical, detail flowing & organic.

XR Guild Hall · Style Onboarding · 02

Look & feel

Palette · black · purple · gold

Black ground, Guild-purple identity, gold accent. Cast-gold tracery is the only glowing material (#EEC96B). Labradorite adds its emerald & sapphire flash. The full iridescent spectrum is posters & print only — never an in-world fill.

Sky & light

Golden-hour sunset meets aurora night sky — the same emerald-blue-purple-gold as the labradorite, thrown across the sky. A sky shifter may offer more than one day/night mood. Serene and alive — never gloomy or neon.

Materials

Wood carries, gold marks, stone holds the light. Quartersawn oak, cast-gold tracery, cream marble, patinated bronze, leaded jewel glass, labradorite — near-black until the light moves.

Type · three faces

Marcellus SC — headings, plaques, hero lines (never a paragraph). Oswald — eyebrows, badges, button labels (caps). Poppins — everything read as a sentence.
