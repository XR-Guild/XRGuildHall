Share slides to the Guild Hall

Open this page on a laptop or desktop (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). Your shared window appears on the big screen above the stage once a Guild admin sets it to Live: screenshare. Only your screen is sent: no camera, and no microphone. Speak from inside the Hall.

Tip: share a single window (your slides app in presenter mode), not the whole screen, so notifications stay private. Tick "Share tab audio" to send a video's sound.
