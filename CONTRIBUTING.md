# Contributing to XR Guild Hall

Welcome — this is a volunteer build and every pillar, bench, and stained-glass pane is someone's contribution. Here's how to jump in.

## Find something to do

- Browse the [Project board](https://github.com/LightLodges/XRGuildHall/projects) or [PRODUCTION.md](docs/project/PRODUCTION.md).
- New here? Filter for the **`good-first-task`** label.
- Want a specific kind of work? Filter by category: `architectural`, `production-spaces`, `interactive`, `content`, `gardens`.

## Claim it

1. Comment on the issue to say you're taking it, and **assign yourself**.
2. Move the card to **In progress** on the board.

Claiming out loud keeps two people from building the same reflecting pool.

## Build & submit

- Branch from `main`: `git checkout -b category/short-desc` (e.g. `interactive/join-poster`).
- Keep commits small and legible.
- Open a PR that references the issue: put `Closes #NN` in the description.
- Move the card to **Review** and request one reviewer.

## Definition of done

- Meets house style (gold tracery on dark, Marcellus SC caps) where visual.
- Meets accessibility (seated-reachable, gaze / single-button).
- Reviewed **in-headset** by someone other than you.
- Merged to `main`.

## Ethics & honesty

Join / Donate / event actions follow the no-dark-patterns rule — honest, disclosed, easy to back out of. If a touchpoint asks something of a visitor, it says so plainly.

## Stuck?

Drop it in the **Blocked** lane with a note, and bring it to the Monday sync (Mondays 1:00 PM PST, Discord — see [docs/MEETINGS.md](docs/MEETINGS.md)).
